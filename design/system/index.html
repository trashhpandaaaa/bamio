Principles

Five rules that decide the close calls.

Volt marks the moment.

Volt is for what is live, what is selected, and the one action that matters most on a screen. Never decoration, never status, never text on paper.

Video sits on night.

Anything that shows footage is dark and untinted, in every theme, so creators judge color and exposure accurately.

Loud type, quiet chrome.

Bricolage carries the personality. Controls stay calm, consistent and predictable.

Show what the AI did.

AI work carries the tilted frame and a plain verb, and it can always be edited or undone. No sparkles, no magic.

Talk like a creator.

Second person, sentence case, verbs first, numbers as numerals, no exclamation marks.

Motion

Quick and springy, never floaty. Every animation shows feedback, a change of state, or the order things happen in. With reduced motion on, states still change but nothing moves.

Signature: caption pop

3 tricks in 10 seconds

Words pop in on the beat of the voice-over with --ease-pop. The spoken word gets the volt highlight. This is the one place Bamio overshoots.
